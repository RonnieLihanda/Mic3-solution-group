The problem

The team found government tenders by hand and spent meeting after meeting breaking each one down. Files sat on individual laptops. There was no single source of truth, no templates, no written procedures and no clear tracking. One proposal took two to three weeks, and none were being won.

What we built

  • Gmail alerts that catch new tenders and send them straight into Jira.
  • A Jira pipeline that tracks every tender: new, SME review, management review.
  • Google Drive and Dropbox as one organised home for every file, with standard templates.
  • Claude and ChatGPT to analyse each tender, extract the key requirements and write a first draft.
  • A person reviews every draft. A human stays in the loop at every stage.

The result

More proposals submitted meant more chances to win. The pipeline went from winning nothing to a 33% win rate, and the same pipeline now runs for several companies at once. Because a human checks each step, it has not broken in use.

Handover

We wrote step-by-step procedures and ran about three days of training. After that, anyone in the company could run the pipeline. Total time from first analysis to production: about four weeks.
