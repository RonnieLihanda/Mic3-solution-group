The problem

The team found government tenders by hand and spent meeting after meeting breaking each one down. Files sat on individual laptops. There was no single source of truth, no templates, no written procedures and no clear tracking. One proposal took two to three weeks, about six were handled a month, and none were being won.

Before and after

The same work, with the repetitive steps automated and a person in charge of every decision.
